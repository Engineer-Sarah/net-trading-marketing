The Company

Direct-from-source supply, backed by hands-on engineering.

Established in 2000 in the UAE, Net Trading & Marketing expanded operations to Rawalpindi & Islamabad in 2008. We're a trusted provider of general order supplies, business equipment, printing services, IT & networking, power systems and security surveillance — built on a strong commitment to quality, reliability and customer satisfaction.

Guided by loyalty, honesty, integrity, reliability and mutual respect, we build long-term, trust-based relationships with customers, suppliers and employees alike.
